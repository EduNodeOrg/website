Learn blockchain development and practical IT skills

EduNode is an educational platform for becoming a blockchain developer. Hands-on courses cover Stellar, Web3, Soroban smart contracts, Ethereum, and Hyperledger, alongside self-paced IT masterclasses in Python, SQL, JavaScript, AI workflow automation, cybersecurity, networking, Docker, Git, and data analysis.

What is EduNode?

A learning platform that combines written lessons, interactive coding challenges, quizzes, and verifiable certificates. It is built for beginners who want practical, project-based paths into blockchain and IT careers.

What can you learn?

  • IT masterclasses — ten paths, 100 lessons: Python, SQL, JavaScript, Git, AI automation, cybersecurity, networking, Docker, data analysis, and IT support
  • Blockchain courses — Stellar, Web3, Soroban, Ethereum, and Hyperledger fundamentals
  • Guides and articles — wallets, smart contract security, DeFi, tokenization, and developer roadmaps

How much is free?

The first two lessons of every masterclass (20%) are free without an account. The remaining lessons and final projects are included with a Pro membership.
